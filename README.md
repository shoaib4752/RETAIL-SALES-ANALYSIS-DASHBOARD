# 🛒 Retail Sales Analysis

An end-to-end Retail Sales Analysis project developed using **Power BI, SQL, DAX, Power Query, and PostgreSQL**. This project transforms raw retail sales data into interactive dashboards that help analyze business performance, product sales, customer behavior, and store insights for better decision-making.

---

# 📌 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance
- Track product category and product-level performance
- Monitor customer and store insights
- Identify top-performing products and stores
- Build an interactive business intelligence dashboard

---

# 🛠 Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- SQL
- PostgreSQL
- Power Query
- DAX
- CSV Dataset

# 📈 Business Insights

- Electronics generated the highest sales revenue.
- Home & Furniture delivered the highest profit.
- A small number of products contributed a significant share of total sales.
- Most orders were successfully completed.
- Credit Card was the most preferred payment method.
- Regular customers generated the highest revenue.
- Sales varied across different states.
- A few stores consistently outperformed others.
- Monthly sales showed seasonal fluctuations.
- Product profitability differed significantly across categories.

---

# 💡 Business Recommendations

- Increase inventory for high-selling products.
- Promote high-profit product categories.
- Improve performance of low-performing products.
- Launch targeted campaigns for Premium and VIP customers.
- Reward loyal customers through loyalty programs.
- Optimize inventory planning using sales trends.
- Expand successful store strategies to other locations.
- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing states.
- Improve return management to reduce cancelled orders.
- Monitor product profitability regularly.
